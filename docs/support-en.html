Support and help

The app is free, with no ads or in-app purchases. Quran pages are available offline; recitation downloads are optional.

Common questions

How do I show the controls and select a verse?

Long-press a verse to reveal the controls and select it. Recitation starts only when you press the upper play button.

How do I download or remove recitation?

Open Settings → Download Manager. Download a surah or the complete recitation; the total size is shown before confirming a full download. Remove downloaded audio from the same screen. Pages, personal notes and marks remain on your device.

What do the two play buttons do?

The upper play button controls recitation. The lower play button controls automatic scrolling in landscape. Their speeds can be adjusted independently.

How do I add a note or mark?

Open the marking tools with the pen button. Choose underline or highlight, or tap i and then a verse-number symbol to add a text or voice note. Pause automatic scrolling before marking.

Where are my saved marks and notes?

Open the index to view saved marks and notes and jump to their locations. Swipe an entry horizontally to reveal its delete button.

How do I change language or open the guide?

Open Settings and choose العربية or English from the language menu. The illustrated user guide is also available in Settings.

Where is the completion supplication?

Open the index and choose the Quran completion supplication. It is displayed separately from the Quran pages.

What if a download stops?

Check your internet connection and available storage, keep the app visible while downloading, and retry in Download Manager. Completed downloads can be played offline.
