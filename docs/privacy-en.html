Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

The app requires no account. We do not include advertising, advertising-tracking tools or analytics SDKs in the app. Optional audio downloads involve the hosting services described below.

Notes, voice recordings, highlights, bookmarks, reading position and settings are stored on your device. The app does not send them to the developer. Device backups may include this data according to your Apple settings.

The microphone is used only to record a voice note with your permission. Deleting a note also deletes its associated recording.

Recitation downloads are optional. When you request a download, the audio source receives the file request and the network address needed to deliver it. Your notes are not sent. Downloaded audio is excluded from backups and can be removed in Downloads.

Audio is hosted on GitHub. GitHub and its delivery providers receive your network address and download requests, and may retain technical data under their privacy policies. GitHub’s policy describes IP-based approximate location, request and device information, service statistics, security and troubleshooting. These technical records may be associated with your network or device; we do not promise that the host anonymizes them before storage. Our App Store disclosure includes coarse location, download-related usage and diagnostic data on that conservative basis. The app does not request GPS location or send an advertising identifier. No GitHub account is required. Your notes, recordings and bookmarks are not uploaded.

Support email and website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and investigate your request. You do not need to send private notes or recordings to get help.

This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The host may log your IP address and technical request information under GitHub’s privacy policy. We do not add analytics, advertising or data-collection forms to this website.

You can delete notes and marks in the app, remove audio in Download Manager, and revoke microphone permission in iOS Settings. For privacy questions or requests about support messages you have sent, contact sshouiki@gmail.com.
